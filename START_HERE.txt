SPEKTRAFILM CAMERA LAB - PHASE 1 DEVELOPMENT RELEASE, NOT CAMERA READY

The goal is Kodak Portra 160 on Nikon Zf and Fujifilm X100V, measured
against Andrea Volpato's SpektraFilm (not Fuji X Weekly or another recipe).

WHAT IS READY:
- Software test suite (51 tests passed locally; CI also passes)
- Native Nikon NP3 file-format diagnostic tests; Fujifilm X100V diagnostic settings
- RAW -> provisional scene-linear ProPhoto converter for .NEF/.RAF
- SpektraFilm reference wrapper; reproducible one-shot RAW processing command
- Image comparison, color measurement, camera-response fitting tools
- Physical X100V C6 RAW/JPEG diagnostics, including one held-out scene
- Source stock queue includes Portra 400, Vision3 250D, Ektar 100,
  Portra 800, Vision3 50D, Vision3 500T, and Premier 2393 print variants

WHAT IS NOT READY:
- No actual SpektraFilm Portra 160 camera recipe has been validated.
- No file here is a validated Portra 160 Nikon Picture Control.
- No Fuji X100V custom bank has been fitted to Portra 160.
- The X100V has been photographed, but its custom-bank properties were not
  independently read back; Nikon Zf hardware remains untested.

READ README.md for setup and the commands. Check real status:
    python3 -m portra160_lab.cli phase1-status --workspace .
  or, after installation:
    portra160-lab phase1-status --workspace .

Do not overwrite your own Nikon or Fuji custom settings with diagnostic files
unless you have first backed them up and are prepared for an experimental test.
